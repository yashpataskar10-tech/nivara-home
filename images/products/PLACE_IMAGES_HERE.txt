Real product photography goes in this folder.

Each product in js/products-data.js points at a specific filename here,
matching its SKU in lowercase, e.g.:

  NH-W01  ->  images/products/nh-w01.jpg
  NH-W02  ->  images/products/nh-w02.jpg
  NH-W03  ->  images/products/nh-w03.jpg
  NH-L01  ->  images/products/nh-l01.jpg
  NH-L02  ->  images/products/nh-l02.jpg
  NH-A01  ->  images/products/nh-a01.jpg
  NH-A02  ->  images/products/nh-a02.jpg
  NH-A03  ->  images/products/nh-a03.jpg
  NH-S01  ->  images/products/nh-s01.jpg
  NH-S02  ->  images/products/nh-s02.jpg

Recommended: square photos, at least 1000x1000px, on a plain or
lightly styled background consistent with the warm, minimal brand
look described in the design brief.

Until a real photo is added, every page automatically falls back to
a generated placeholder graphic (see placeholderImage() in
js/main.js), so the site never shows a broken-image icon — you can
add photos one at a time without breaking anything.

If you'd also like a lifestyle/hero photo, add one and update the
`src` set in the inline scripts of index.html (#hero-image) and
(#lifestyle-image).
